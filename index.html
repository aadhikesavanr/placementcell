How it works

Three roles, one queue, no back-and-forth emails.

The cell reviews everything once. If a posting needs changes, the recruiter gets the reason in writing — and it shows up in the audit log.

For students

Apply only to what you are eligible for

Sign in, fill your branch, CGPA and resume link, then browse approved openings. Eligibility is checked against the posting's cut-off before you can submit — if your CGPA is 7.2 and the role asks for 7.5, the portal tells you exactly that.

For recruiters

Post once, track every applicant

Create your company profile, submit the role with package, departments and cut-off, then wait for approval. After that you can shortlist, schedule interviews and release offers — individually or in a batch.

For the placement cell

Approve, reject with a reason, keep the record

Companies and openings sit in separate queues. Approvals and rejections are written to an audit log with reviewer ID, reason and timestamp — searchable when a department asks who cleared what.
